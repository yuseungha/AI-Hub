# AI icons

- `claude.svg`: unmodified Claude Spark (Clay), Anthropic official media resources, https://www.anthropic.com/press-kit (downloaded 2026-11-14).
- `codex.svg`: Codex colour icon, Lobe Icons, https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/packages/static-svg/icons/codex-color.svg.
- `chatgpt.svg`: OpenAI monochrome Blossom, Lobe Icons, https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/master/packages/static-svg/icons/openai.svg.
- Lobe Icons source licence is included in `LOBE-ICONS-LICENSE.txt`. Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners; icons identify the corresponding services in this personal local app.
- Matching PNGs are raster exports of the original SVGs for Qt rich-text rendering. User-selected accent colours apply to labels and UI decorations, not the marks themselves.
